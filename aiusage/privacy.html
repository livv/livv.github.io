Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

AI Usage (the "App", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Cursor usage tracking app on Apple devices.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed to keep data local and minimal:

  • Cursor Cookie: You provide a Cursor cookie so the App can request usage data from Cursor's endpoints. We store it locally in the Keychain and mirror a copy in the App Group so the widget can read it on device.
  • Usage Snapshots: The App caches usage snapshots, billing analysis results, and related metadata on your device to support quick viewing and widget refreshes.
  • Diagnostics: Error messages may be shown locally to help you understand a failed refresh. We do not build advertising profiles or sell personal data.

2. How We Use Information

Any information processed by the App is used only to:

  • Fetch and display Cursor usage information
  • Generate billing cycle analysis and model ranking views
  • Keep the widget and menu bar views up to date on your devices
  • Help diagnose local sync or refresh issues

3. Storage and Security

We keep your data on device whenever possible:

  • Local Storage: Cookie data is stored in the Keychain; cached snapshots are written locally.
  • App Group Mirroring: A mirrored cookie and shared snapshot are used so the widget can refresh without sending data to our servers.
  • No Cloud Backend: We do not maintain our own cloud service for storing your usage history.

4. Third-Party Services

The App communicates with Cursor's services to fetch the usage data tied to your own account. System services such as Keychain, WidgetKit, and App Group storage are also used. We do not include third-party advertising SDKs.

5. Your Choices

You can control the App's local data at any time:

  • Clear the saved cookie in Settings
  • Uninstall the App to remove local data from your devices
  • Stop using the widget or menu bar features if you no longer want background refreshes

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document, and the date below will be updated accordingly.

本应用以本地化和最小化数据处理为原则：

  • Cursor Cookie：您需要提供 Cursor Cookie，以便应用向 Cursor 的接口请求用量数据。我们会将其保存在本地钥匙串，并在 App Group 中镜像一份，供小组件在本机读取。
  • 用量快照：应用会在设备上缓存用量快照、计费分析结果及相关元数据，以支持快速查看和小组件刷新。
  • 诊断信息：刷新失败时，应用可能在本地显示错误信息，帮助您排查问题。我们不会构建广告画像，也不会出售个人数据。

2. 我们如何使用信息

应用处理到的信息仅用于以下目的：

  • 获取并展示 Cursor 用量信息
  • 生成计费周期分析和模型排行视图
  • 让小组件和菜单栏视图在设备上保持最新
  • 帮助诊断本地同步或刷新问题

我们尽可能让数据留在设备本地：

  • 本地存储：Cookie 数据保存在钥匙串中；缓存快照写入本地。
  • App Group 镜像：应用会使用镜像 Cookie 和共享快照，让小组件无需把数据发送到我们的服务器即可刷新。
  • 无云端后端：我们不维护用于存储您用量历史的云端服务。

本应用会与 Cursor 的服务通信，以获取属于您账号的用量数据。同时也会使用钥匙串、WidgetKit 和 App Group 存储等系统服务。我们不包含任何第三方广告 SDK。

5. 您的选择

您可随时控制本应用的本地数据：

  • 在设置中清除已保存的 Cookie
  • 卸载应用以移除设备上的本地数据
  • 如果不再希望后台刷新，可停止使用小组件或菜单栏功能
