Privacy Policy

How we handle your data

My Homework (the “App”) is an iPhone application for parents and students to record and share school homework. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is stored, and your choices. We do not operate our own backend servers for the core features of the App.

1. Information the App processes

Depending on how you use the App, it may process:

  • Homework records: dates, subjects, task titles, notes, completion status, and sort order you enter or confirm after pasting text.
  • Attachments: photos or files you attach to homework days or tasks, stored on your device and optionally in your iCloud account when sync is enabled.
  • Subjects and settings: custom subject names, colors, default scores, role (parent or student), and other preferences saved locally.
  • Pasted text: content you copy from messaging apps (such as WeChat group messages) solely to parse and create homework entries inside the App.

2. Local storage and iCloud / CloudKit

By default, your homework data is stored on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device storage. If you sign in to iCloud and use CloudKit sync or family sharing in the App, related data may sync between your devices or with participants you invite through Apple’s CloudKit sharing, subject to Apple’s iCloud terms and security practices. We cannot access your Apple ID password or the contents of your private iCloud account.

3. Family sharing between parent and student

When a parent shares homework with a student, Apple’s CloudKit share invitation flow is used. Only people you invite can access the shared homework zone. Accepting or declining an invitation is handled by Apple’s system UI. We do not receive your messages or contacts; sharing is initiated from your device.

4. Widgets

If you add the Home Screen widget, it displays a limited summary of homework data from storage Apple provides for the App and its widget extension (such as today’s tasks). Widget data stays on your device and in your iCloud sync scope when enabled.

5. Analytics, advertising, and sale of data

The App is not designed to show third-party advertising. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use an independent analytics SDK to track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

6. Children, parents, and schools

The App is intended for parents and students to manage homework on devices they control. The adult who owns the device and Apple account is responsible for invitations, sharing, and what information is entered. The App is not a school-managed system and does not replace official school platforms.

7. Your choices

  • You can edit or delete homework, subjects, and attachments inside the App where supported.
  • You can stop sharing or leave a CloudKit share through the App or iOS settings.
  • You can sign out of iCloud or disable sync for the App in system settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its local data from the device; data already in iCloud follows Apple’s sync and deletion behavior for your account.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date below will be revised accordingly.

我的作业（「本应用」）是一款供家长与学生记录、共享学校作业的 iPhone 应用。本隐私政策说明本应用处理哪些信息、如何存储以及您有哪些选择。本应用的核心功能不依赖我们自行运营的后端服务器。

1. 本应用处理的信息

视您的使用方式，本应用可能处理：

  • 作业记录：日期、科目、任务标题、备注、完成状态及排序等您录入或粘贴解析后确认的内容。
  • 附件：您为作业日或任务添加的照片或文件，保存在设备上，并在启用同步时可能存入您的 iCloud 账户。
  • 科目与设置：自定义科目名称、颜色、默认分值、角色（家长/学生）等保存在本地的偏好。
  • 粘贴文本：您从即时通讯应用（如微信群消息）复制的内容，仅用于在本应用内解析并生成作业条目。

2. 本地存储与 iCloud / CloudKit

默认情况下，作业数据保存在您的 iPhone 上，并使用 Apple 的设备端存储技术。若您登录 iCloud 并在本应用中使用 CloudKit 同步或家庭共享，相关数据可能通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 在您的设备间或与经您邀请的参与者之间同步，并受 Apple 的 iCloud 条款与安全实践约束。我们无法访问您的 Apple ID 密码或您私人 iCloud 账户中的内容。

3. 家长与学生的家庭共享

家长向学生分享作业时，使用 Apple 的 CloudKit 分享邀请流程。仅被您邀请的人员可访问共享的作业数据区。接受或拒绝邀请由 Apple 系统界面处理。我们不会接收您的聊天记录或通讯录；分享由您在设备上发起。

4. 小组件

若您添加主屏幕小组件，将展示来自本应用及其小组件扩展可用存储中的有限作业摘要（如今日任务）。小组件数据保留在您的设备上，并在启用同步时处于您的 iCloud 同步范围内。

5. 分析、广告与数据出售

本应用不以展示第三方广告为目的设计。我们不出售您的个人信息，也不使用独立的第三方分析 SDK 在其他公司的应用或网站间对您进行广告追踪。

6. 儿童、家长与学校

本应用供家长与学生在各自控制的设备上管理作业。设备与 Apple 账户的成年持有人对邀请、共享及录入内容负责。本应用不是学校官方系统，不能替代学校管理平台。

7. 您的选择

  • 您可在应用内编辑或删除作业、科目与附件（在功能支持范围内）。
  • 您可通过本应用或 iOS 设置停止共享或退出 CloudKit 共享。
  • 您可在系统设置中退出 iCloud 或调整本应用的同步行为。
  • 卸载本应用会移除设备上的本地数据；已位于 iCloud 的数据遵循您账户下 Apple 的同步与删除规则。

8. 政策变更

当本应用或法律要求变化时，我们可能更新本隐私政策。下方的「最后更新」日期将随之修订。
